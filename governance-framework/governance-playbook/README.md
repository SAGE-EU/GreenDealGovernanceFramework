# Governance Playbook

The Governance Playbook outlines the Organisational Form, Governance Model, and Governance Authority, defining the structural foundation and decision-making mechanisms essential for the effective governance and operation of the GDDS.&#x20;

These were developed through an iterative, research-informed, and co-creative process. It reflects both established practice in existing European data spaces and the specific needs identified with the consortium and use case partners through the SAGE co-creation process. As a result, the SAGE consortium proposed a governance model for the GDDS that is responsive, transparent, and representative of its participants.

Readers seeking to understand the governance bodies, their mandates, and how authority and decision-making are distributed across the data space should start here; the detailed rules these bodies define and enforce are set out in the GDDS Rulebook section that follows.
