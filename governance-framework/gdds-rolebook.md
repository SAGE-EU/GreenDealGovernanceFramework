# GDDS Rolebook

{% hint style="warning" %}
_The GDDS Rolebook is envisioned as a companion document to the Green Deal Data Space (GDDS) Rulebook. Where the Rulebook establishes the overall governance structure, legal framework, and participation lifecycle, the Rolebook would operationalise those rules into role-specific playbooks._

_For each GDDS participant role, the Rolebook would define what that role is, what the participant holding it is permitted to do, what they are obligated to do, what technical requirements they must meet, and what service level standards apply to them._

_The Rolebook would not replace the Rulebook. It would complement it._&#x20;
{% endhint %}
