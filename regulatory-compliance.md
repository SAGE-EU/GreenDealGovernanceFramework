# Regulatory Compliance

Regulatory compliance within the Green Deal Data Space (GDDS) is aligned with the objectives of the European Data Strategy and the development of Common European Data Spaces, which aim to enable trustworthy and interoperable data sharing across organisations and sectors. In this context, the GDDS supports participants in identifying, understanding, and addressing the legal and regulatory requirements applicable to their data-sharing activities, including through guidance, checklists, conformity assessment tools, and other resources.

Rather than setting out legal requirements itself, the GDDS provides a framework to help participants navigate the relevant EU and national legislation relating to their data-sharing activities. This covers areas such as data protection, cybersecurity, intellectual property, trade secrets, sustainability and competition, including those relating to discriminatory access, governance neutrality, and the avoidance of inappropriate exchanges of competitively sensitive information. The GDDS does not provide legal advice, determine participants’ legal obligations, or replace their responsibility for compliance. Responsibility for complying with applicable legislation remains with each participant. Instead, the GDDS seeks to promote a common understanding of relevant requirements and support their consistent implementation within the data space ecosystem.

Recognising that the regulatory landscape continues to evolve, the GDDS Rulebook is intended to be updated over time to reflect changes in applicable legislation, regulatory guidance, and best practices, while preserving stability and interoperability for participants wherever possible. By integrating compliance considerations into its governance framework, the GDDS supports transparency, trust, accountability, and responsible data-sharing practices, helping participants manage legal and operational risks while contributing to the objectives of the European Green Deal.

Beyond formal legal compliance, an additional strategic objective of the GDDS is to ensure that compliance measures are coherently aligned with the GDDS Values, Ethics Principles and the Governance and Technical requirements of the Data Space. Compliance is therefore not conceived as a purely legal or administrative exercise, but as an integrated design principle that informs governance structures, operational procedures, and technical implementation of choices. Lastly, this ensures that data-sharing practices within the GDDS not only meet regulatory thresholds but also embody the broader values underpinning the European Green Deal and the European Data Strategy.

On one hand, these measures should recognise the various rights and interests relating to data, which may arise from data protection law, intellectual property rights, contractual arrangements or sector-specific legislation. In this context, an appropriate balance should be maintained between openness and confidentiality: while broad data accessibility can support innovation, evidence-based policymaking and sustainability objectives, participants remain responsible for protecting their legitimate interests and fulfilling their legal obligations. The GDDS governance framework therefore provides common rules and guidance concerning data access, use, re-use, and stewardship, while respecting participants’ autonomy and responsibilities.

On the other hand, compliance considerations should be reflected in the technical architecture of the GDDS through “compliance by design” principles, including well-known concepts such as data protection by design and by default where applicable (GDPR Article 25). This includes alignment with data access controls, identity and access management mechanisms, interoperability standards, traceability systems, audit functionalities, and secure data processing environments. By integrating legal, governance and policy requirements into technical and operational processes, the GDDS aims to support participants in managing compliance obligations and strengthening trust across the ecosystem.
