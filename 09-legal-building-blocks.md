# Introduction

The Legal Framework of the GDDS establish the legal basis for participation in and operation of the data space, enabling trustworthy, compliant, and scalable data sharing across diverse actors and sectors. It is aligned with the objectives of the [European Data Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/strategy-data) and the development of [Common European Data Spaces](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-spaces), which seek to facilitate trusted data sharing while ensuring compliance with applicable legal requirements.&#x20;

The Legal Framework supports legal certainty by helping stakeholders understand and apply the relevant regulatory obligations and by providing a structured set of contractual arrangements and rules for collaboration. These arrangements are designed to support a data space in which multiple independent participants share and use data under common governance principles while retaining control over their own data, services, and legal responsibilities.&#x20;

This pillar is composed of two key components:

* **Regulatory Compliance,** which supports participants in understanding and addressing the legal requirements applicable to their activities in the data space. This includes providing guidance, checklists, conformity assessment tools and other resources that help participants demonstrate and maintain compliance with applicable legislation. The GDDS does not enforce legal obligations or replace participants’ responsibility for compliance. Rather, it provides a framework to help participants meet those obligations in a consistent and transparent manner. The GDDS Rulebook complements, but does not replace, sector-specific regulatory frameworks and is designed to accommodate external oversight and compliance processes where required;
* **Contractual Framework,** which defines the legal and contractual arrangements required for participation in the GDDS, including governance, participation, and data-sharing agreements. These agreements establish the rights, responsibilities, and liabilities of participants and provide the legal foundation for trusted collaboration within the data space.

Overall, these components help reduce transaction costs, promote legal interoperability, and create a reliable basis for sustainable data sharing and collaboration in support of the European Green Deal. Stakeholders seeking to understand the regulatory requirements and contractual relationships underpinning participation in the GDDS will find the relevant guidance in this section.
