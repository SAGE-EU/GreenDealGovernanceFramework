# Trust & Participation Governance

The Trust & Participation Governance defines how organisations and individuals become participants in the Green Deal Data Space and how their rights, responsibilities, and access privileges are governed throughout the lifecycle of their participation.&#x20;

Participation in GDDS is open to organisations that commit to the governance framework and its Rulebook and comply with the eligibility and trust requirements established by the data space. The framework ensures that all actors operating within GDDS are properly identified, verified, and authorised to perform their roles in a secure, transparent, and accountable manner.&#x20;

The purpose of the Trust & Participation Governance is to ensure that all actors operating within the data space are properly identified, verified, and authorised to perform their roles in a secure and transparent manner. It establishes the conditions under which organisations may join the data space, the basis on which they operate within it, and the terms on which their participation may end. Participation management, therefore, spans the following core processes:&#x20;

1. identification of candidate participants&#x20;
2. &#x20;eligibility assessment and admission procedures&#x20;
3. Onboarding, assignment of roles and access rights&#x20;
4. monitoring of participant compliance with governance rules&#x20;
5. suspension, revocation, or voluntary withdrawal procedures&#x20;

By defining clear participation rules and lifecycle processes, the Trust & Participation Governance ensures that data exchange takes place in a trusted environment where responsibilities, permissions, and accountability are clearly defined.&#x20;

The next sections present the lifecycle as a single narrative. The first three stages — identification, eligibility and admission, and onboarding with the assignment of roles and access rights — are outlined below. Each stage below follows the same structure: the applicable rule or criteria, the procedure that applies it, and the body responsible.&#x20;
